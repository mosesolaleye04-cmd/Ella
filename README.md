# MNE 201 CBT — FUTA
### Introduction to Engineering | 215 Questions

A fully offline CBT study app for **MNE 201 (Introduction to Engineering)** at the Federal University of Technology, Akure (FUTA).

Built by **LMD Grafix × ASCEND** for FUTA 200L students.

## Features
- 215 questions across 11 topics
- 4 study modes: Exam, Practice, Quick Fire, Review
- Topic filter — drill specific topics
- 60-second timer per question (Exam mode)
- Instant feedback + end-of-session review
- Fully offline — no internet needed after loading
- Mobile-optimized

## Topics Covered
- History of Science
- History of Engineering
- Industrial Revolutions
- Ages of Man
- Ancient Civilizations
- Nigerian Engineering (COREN, NSE, NCSIR, ITF, etc.)
- Branches of Engineering
- Engineering Professions
- Definitions
- Risk Management
- Engineering Ethics
